# Luma 相機 icon 第一版

設計方向：霧黑底、琥珀光環與香檳白鏡頭，中央帶細微青綠光學反射，呼應光線、攝影與 LUT 色彩。沒有文字與第三方標誌，適合 App 圖示的視覺探索。

工具：內建 `image_gen`，依 `imagegen` 技能產生。成品：`luma-icon-v1.png`。

目前是設計稿，尚未替換 App launcher icon，也沒有操作手機。正式 Android 適配時應另製作滿版背景、透明 foreground、monochrome 圖層，並驗證圓形與其他 launcher 遮罩及小尺寸辨識。

## 原始提示詞

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: a finished app icon design for Luma, an Android camera app for photography, live LUT film color filters and video, with a minimal iOS-inspired camera interface. Create ONE exceptionally polished original app icon, square canvas, centered, no presentation board or multiple concepts. Edge-to-edge deep matte charcoal background with very subtly rounded app tile silhouette and no exterior margin. The main mark is a bold minimal circular camera lens / aperture composed of three broad clean nested rings, with a distinctive slightly off-center glowing amber light crescent embedded in the outer ring. The rings should also subtly suggest a small optical prism and the letter L through negative space, but the lens must remain immediately legible at tiny launcher sizes. Main symbol occupies about 56 percent of the canvas width so there is generous adaptive-icon crop safety. Premium restrained optical glass material, ivory and warm amber highlights, with a tiny desaturated teal spectral reflection inside the darkest lens center to suggest LUT film color. Graphic clarity first: large strong shapes, clean smooth geometry, subtle depth, restrained specular highlights, beautiful luminous light, calm luxurious photographic identity. Front-on orthographic view, no perspective, strong contrast, perfectly balanced silhouette. NO text, letters printed on the icon, words, FUJIFILM logos, Apple logos, extra symbols, camera body drawing, scenery, overly complex shutter blades, film perforations, watermark, mockup phone, drop shadow outside the icon, or multicolor rainbow. Deliver one standalone icon artwork, not an icon on a background sheet.
